A few common questions

Good to know.

Otaroo is here to help you notice, not to tell you what to do.

Why doesn’t Otaroo count calories?

Otaroo is designed to help you notice how meals relate to how you feel, rather than quantify your food. It focuses on your notes, qualitative tags, food groups, and gentle patterns—without calorie, gram, or macro counters.

How does the Nutrition Compass support healthy aging?

The Compass gives plain counts of foods and variety you’ve logged across a week, such as vegetables and fruit, beans and lentils, whole grains, and nuts and seeds. It compares weeks without turning the information into a score or a prescription.

Can I use Otaroo offline, and what does AI see?

Yes. You can use Otaroo as a guest and save your journal on your device without an account. AI is opt-in; its helper reads your logs only when you explicitly enable it. You decide whether to keep each suggestion.

How are allergies handled?

Saved allergies and skipped foods are used to filter meal ideas and plans. Please review ingredients and labels before preparing a dish, since products and recipes can vary.

Which languages and devices does Otaroo support?

Otaroo is available for iOS and Android, with English, Spanish, and Filipino food knowledge and downloadable regional packs. Native Home Screen Widgets help you reach your journal from your device.
