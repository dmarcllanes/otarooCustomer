01

Scope & publisher information

This Privacy Policy applies to the Otaroo mobile application for iOS and Android and the public Otaroo website (together, the “Service”). Otaroo is published and operated by Dan Marc Llanes (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

Otaroo is a personal food and feelings journal designed to help you notice how meals relate to how you feel. Otaroo is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, or professional nutrition advice.

02

Information we collect & where it lives

What information is processed depends on how you choose to use Otaroo. You can use the core app entirely offline on your device, or optionally enable cloud backup and AI assistance.

A. Information stored locally on your device (Guest Mode & on-device tools)

When you use Otaroo in Guest Mode, your journal data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Meal journal entries: Meal titles, text notes, qualitative tags (such as protein-rich or fiber-rich), food groups, ingredients, timestamps, and any meal photos you attach locally.
  • Post-meal check-ins: Optional self-reported reflections on your energy, tummy comfort, fullness, mood, focus, and meal enjoyment.
  • Always-local personal settings: Your saved food profile (allergies and skipped foods), curiosity experiments, 2–5 day meal plans, shopping lists, notification preferences, and downloaded regional food knowledge packs remain stored on your device.

B. Optional account & cloud backup data (Sign in with Apple / Google)

Creating an account is optional. If you choose to sign in using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In to back up your journal:

  • Account identifiers: We receive your authentication user ID and email address from Apple or Google (including an Apple Private Relay address if you select “Hide My Email”) to create and authenticate your account.
  • Synced journal data: Your meal journal entries and post-meal check-ins are stored in our cloud database powered by Supabase so you can back up and restore your journal across devices.

C. Optional AI helper data (100% opt-in)

Otaroo’s AI helper is turned off by default. It processes your meal inputs or reads your journal logs only if you explicitly turn it on:

  • Meal suggestions & analysis: When you opt into AI and ask Otaroo to suggest a meal name, qualitative tags, food groups, ingredients, or Food Guide / Otaroo Mentor ideas, the specific input you provide (such as meal text, voice transcript, or a meal photo submitted for recognition) and the journal logs you allow the AI helper to read are sent to our cloud AI service provider solely to generate that response.
  • Human review before saving: AI suggestions are checked against USDA FoodData Central and filtered on your device using your saved allergies and skipped foods. Nothing generated by AI is added to your journal unless you tap Keep.

D. Support & contact communications

If you contact us by email at dandigital1688@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message so we can respond and assist you.

E. Public website

You can browse the Otaroo website without creating an account. The website does not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral analytics. Standard web server request metadata (such as IP address and browser user-agent) is processed transiently to deliver pages securely.

03

Device permissions (iOS & Android)

Otaroo requests operating-system permissions only when you use a feature that requires them. You can grant or revoke any permission at any time in your iOS or Android system settings:

  • Camera & Photo Library (Optional): Used only when you choose to take a photo of a meal or pick an image from your library for a meal entry.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Optional): Used only when you tap the microphone button to log a meal by voice.
  • Face ID, Touch ID & Biometric App Lock (Optional): If you enable App Lock, authentication is performed entirely by your device’s operating system (Apple LocalAuthentication or Android Biometric APIs). Otaroo never accesses, collects, stores, or transmits your biometric templates or raw face or fingerprint data.
  • Local Notifications (Optional): Used only if you enable gentle check-in or meal-logging nudges. Otaroo enforces a calm notification budget of at most three notifications per day across the entire app.
04

How we use your information

We use the information described above strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and display your personal food and feelings journal;
  • Authenticate your optional account and sync or restore your cloud backup when you sign in with Apple or Google;
  • Generate meal tags, food group breakdowns, and meal ideas when you explicitly enable and use opt-in AI features;
  • Deliver signed regional food knowledge packs and open culinary reference data;
  • Respond to support questions, data export inquiries, or account deletion requests; and
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Service.
Our commitment: We never sell, rent, or broker your personal information. We do not use your meal logs, photos, voice notes, allergies, or check-ins for advertising, ad targeting, or data brokering.
05

Third-party service providers & open knowledge

When you enable optional cloud features, Otaroo relies on the following service providers solely to operate those features on your behalf:

  • Supabase (Authentication & Cloud Backup): Hosts account authentication records and encrypted cloud database backups when you choose to sign in.
  • Apple Inc. & Google LLC (Sign-In & App Distribution): Provide optional Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In authentication, native operating-system services, and app distribution through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
  • Cloud AI Service Provider (Opt-In AI Processing): Processes meal descriptions, photos, or allowed journal context only when you explicitly turn on AI features and request AI suggestions.
  • Open Nutrition & Culinary Knowledge Sources: Meal group references and food science curiosities draw from open public datasets including USDA FoodData Central, Wikidata, and Wikipedia.

07

Data retention, export & account deletion

You remain in control of your journal and account data at all times:

  • On-device Guest Mode data: Stored locally on your phone for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you delete entries or clear app storage.
  • Export your data anytime: You can export a copy of your journal directly from the Otaroo app settings at any time.
  • Immediate one-tap in-app account deletion: If you created an account for cloud backup, you can delete your account and all associated cloud backup data immediately in one tap inside Otaroo’s settings.
  • Web / email account deletion requests: If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion outside the app, email dandigital1688@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject line “Delete My Otaroo Account”. We will verify and permanently delete your account credentials and cloud-stored journal data within 30 days.
08

Data security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information:

  • All network communication between the Otaroo app and cloud services (Supabase and opt-in AI endpoints) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Cloud backups in Supabase are isolated per authenticated user account.
  • Downloadable regional food knowledge packs are cryptographically signed so the app can verify their integrity.
  • You can enable Face ID, Touch ID, or Android biometric App Lock to protect local access to your journal on your device.
09

Your privacy rights & choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, the Philippine Data Privacy Act, and similar privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • Access and export a portable copy of the personal data you have logged;
  • Correct or delete individual meal entries, check-ins, or your entire account;
  • Withdraw consent for optional features at any time (such as turning off opt-in AI or signing out / deleting cloud backup); and
  • Use Otaroo in offline Guest Mode without providing an email address or creating an account.

You can exercise these choices directly inside the Otaroo app or by contacting us at dandigital1688@gmail.com. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information through Otaroo, please contact us at dandigital1688@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.
